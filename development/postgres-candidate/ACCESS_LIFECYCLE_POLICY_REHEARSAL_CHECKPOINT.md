## Purpose and boundaries

Turn selected rules in the [personal-access design](PERSONAL_ACCESS_LIFECYCLE_DESIGN.md) into fictional final-check and membership-lifetime examples while Inventory's tested baseline/catalog is pending. Inventory owns the future single migration ledger; Jeff approves shared-schema changes. No SQL, migration 064, shared table, provider call, account, grant, auth route, target read or hosted/Inventory change is added.

The [pure module](access-lifecycle-policy-rehearsal.mjs) is imported only by its [tests](access-lifecycle-policy-rehearsal.test.mjs). The active application, adapters, CLI and previews do not call it. Every outcome is labelled fictional and non-authoritative, with canApply, accessProvisioned and completenessCertified false. Input person/session/lifecycle/owner/response flags are supplied fictional evidence; they verify no real identity, owner roster, provider lifecycle or current authorization. Outputs contain only fixed result codes and flags, not people, proposals or policy data.

## Rehearsed contracts

| Function | Scope | Important exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rehearseOwnerChange | Distinct currently eligible existing owners; exact organization/proposer/proposal/owner-set bindings; target-bound consent or explicit objection; eligible-owner preservation; reason and fictional time/response checks | A passing predicate neither approves nor applies anything. No receipt, audit, outbox, locks, bootstrap, exceptional recovery or provider verification. |
| rehearseMembershipEpochTransition | One declared consecutive employment event for the same durable person/store membership; loss of approved, non-schedule-only eligibility advances the epoch exactly once; re-approval/return from schedule-only retains the advanced epoch | Two arbitrary reads cannot prove uninterrupted eligibility. Trusted writers/lifecycle reconciliation must capture every real lapse. The oracle writes neither epochs nor audit events. |
| rehearseMembershipGrantEpoch | Exact organization/store/person/membership match, current approved non-schedule-only employment, active grant and matching captured epoch | This is the membership-lifetime predicate only, never capability, department, session, actor or full grant authorization. Organization policy and independent source-review designations are outside this predicate. |
| rehearseMembershipGrantCreation | Proposed active grant must match a currently approved, non-schedule-only membership and its current epoch at creation/commit; pending, ended and schedule-only memberships cannot receive pre-staged grants | No grant row, A01 actor authorization, audit or receipt is created. Every future grant writer must enforce this prerequisite in the same coordination as membership changes. |

The owner selected refusal of pre-staged store employment-dependent grants during PR65 review. The creation predicate models that prerequisite only; it adds no actual grant writer.

Owner fixtures carry the current final-check clock and an explicit proposal validity setting. null validity, or an unselected removal-response policy, refuses with owner_policy_pending. The tests' short timestamps/duration are wholly fictional examples, not selected production defaults. A05 must still select proposal validity, target-notification/response requirements and exceptional procedures before dependent implementation. A selected response policy with unsatisfied requirements refuses even if the target is silent. These supplied flags are not proof of notification delivery.

A removal target can explicitly consent as the second approver, but the proposer and approver must be different canonical people. A proposed new owner cannot supply the required existing-owner approval. Voluntary self-removal requires another owner. With three owners, A proposing removal of C and B approving can satisfy the distinct-owner predicate while C is silent; a verified recorded objection by C instead holds ordinary removal for A05. A consent/objection for a different proposal, revision or target is refused. No target response is inferred from silence. Single-owner addition and last-owner removal are held, without an override.

A verified recorded objection holds ordinary removal even when the target is currently ineligible. Eligibility loss does not erase the objection; consent can supply the second approval only when that person remains an eligible existing owner. This intentionally treats an objection hold differently from approval authority. The oracle carries one targetResponse (or null for silence); it models neither response history nor withdrawal. It has no eligible-at-recording field and authorizes no new response submission by an ineligible person. The shared port must define authenticated response acceptance, immutable history and any withdrawal/replacement semantics before adding such commands; do not interpret this fixture slot as permission to silently erase an objection.

The owner check is a predicate over a fictional final snapshot, not a simulation of PostgreSQL isolation or atomic approval/application. Its before/after objection and expiry cases show what a future final check must detect; they do not prove concurrent locking, transaction rollback or notification timing. The shared port must implement the atomic approval/mutation/audit/receipt contract from PR64 and recheck current state after locks.

Membership fixtures now carry separate approved and scheduleOnly booleans. Operational employment eligibility is approved && !scheduleOnly; a scheduling-only membership may remain approved for scheduling purposes without operational authority. Ending employment or moving an eligible member into schedule-only advances the epoch without changing stable IDs. Re-approval never resets it; old grants remain ineligible until explicitly reviewed and re-granted at the current epoch by A01 authority. A passing epoch transition does not itself create that audit or authorize the re-grant.

Grant creation requires current approved, non-schedule-only eligibility and the current epoch, so a pending/ended/schedule-only membership cannot receive a grant that wakes up on later activation. Rehire or return from schedule-only needs a new explicit A01 grant review at that point. The epoch read predicate assumes persisted grants passed this creation guard; it cannot prove a fabricated supplied grant was validly created. Future shared writes must coordinate creation and final eligibility checks with membership changes and record the grant review/audit atomically. Another store's membership/epoch remains independent. No-op and eligibility-preserving edits do not increment it; counter exhaustion refuses rather than wrapping.
