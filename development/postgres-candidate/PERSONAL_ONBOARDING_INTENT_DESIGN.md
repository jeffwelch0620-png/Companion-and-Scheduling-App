Setup-issuer authority is an explicitly registered store permission administered under A01 by owners/currently enabled admin; a GM title or source-review designation does not imply it. A store issuer is limited to already approved, non-schedule-only people in that store, and cannot enumerate unrelated people, issue for another store or use that role for non-employed organization actors. Store employment-dependent issuer grants follow the membership epoch and no-pre-staging rules. Organization owners/admin may use their separate organization policy to onboard explicitly reviewed non-employed owners or external reviewers without fabricated employment. Issuer authority still needs its own implementation and current-state checks; no permission name or default role grant is installed by this document.

Self-increases remain bounded by A01's registered capabilities, exact scopes, approved non-schedule-only employment at grant creation and the current membership epoch where employment-dependent. A self-grant cannot create an owner seat, enable the administrator switch, create a source-certification designation or bypass any command's consent/independence rules. schedule.correct stays explicitly granted, initially to nobody; authoring a correction to one's own shift still needs a different authorized person to approve. Organization policy authority is not expanded by an operational self-grant, and an ordinary manager gains no grant-administration power. Required reason/audit applies to every permitted self-increase; this selects no silent default grant.

## Admin self-grant provenance and suspension

Record the canonical grant actor/target, exact capability/scope increment, authority basis at approval (owner or enabled-admin), source grant/delta revision and admin-enablement epoch. An admin-derived self-grant is one whose actor and target are the same canonical person and whose authority basis was enabled-admin; alternative accounts/memberships cannot disguise that identity. An owner-authorized grant remains owner-based even if that person also holds admin status. Only current verified owner authority can create an owner-based approval; an actor cannot relabel an admin-derived grant after the fact.

An owner disabling admin advances its monotonic enablement epoch and atomically removes the admin policy path, suspends the admin-derived self-granted additions/increases and audits the affected provenance/scope. Current permission checks reject these suspended components immediately. If a grant began with an independently owner-approved scope and the admin later enlarged it for themselves, retain that owner-approved baseline while suspending only the admin-derived increment. Preserve the historical grant/audit rather than deleting it. Grants to other canonical people and independent source designations retain their separately reviewed rules.

Re-enablement, new receipts/IDs, rewritten role templates or self-reissuing an overlapping capability/scope must not bypass the suspended permission extent. Preserve a semantic suspension obligation alongside its provenance until a current owner explicitly reconfirms the exact scope under owner authority. Owner reconfirmation rechecks current target eligibility, membership epoch, capability/scope and expected revisions, records reason/audit and creates an owner-approved basis; it is not implied by enabling admin again. The shared writer must coordinate the complete affected store/person scope set under the reviewed organization-policy lock order. Concurrent disable/self-increase/reconfirmation must follow that shared coordination and current-state rechecks, so stale admin authority cannot commit around disablement. No grant or suspension is implemented here.

## Intent envelope and separate state axes

The future app-owned intent binds organization, approved canonical person UUID, optional store/membership scope, immutable intent/receipt identity, initiating actor, reason, exact payload digest, relevant person/link/membership/input revisions and issuer authority/eligibility fence. Store issuer scope is rechecked against its current grant and employment epoch; a source email/name, Toast GUID or claimed manager role cannot select the human. Record setup approval hash/validity/attempt metadata separately from provider operation references and reviewed link evidence. At issuance capture the pre-existing contact record reference/revision, recording actor/time and protected comparison context, where the contact-evidence route will be used. Record the first verified provider identity lock and its binding revision separately from later proof refreshes and link decisions. Do not store raw credentials, setup secrets, provider access/refresh tokens or credential snapshots in the intent, receipt, audit or source archive.

The following are logical state axes, not installed columns or endpoint names. Keeping them separate prevents expiry/revocation from erasing an unresolved provider side effect.

| Axis | Proposed states / meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Setup approval | proposed, approved, consumed, expired, revoked; one-use approval targets the exact reviewed person/context. |
| Provider operation | not_started, pending, unknown, confirmed, refused; unknown is a timeout/ambiguous outcome, never proof of absence. |
| Link review | not_requested, pending, linked, refused; confirmed provider proof alone does not commit a person link. |
| Reconciliation | none, required, resolved; may remain required even after setup expiry/revocation. Resolution needs supported verified provider evidence and current authority. |

Provider/intent/approval versions and semantic revisions remain distinct from routine token refresh or unrelated workspace activity. The shared implementation must capture trusted lifecycle continuity; fictional supplied flags from the rehearsals are never a substitute. Current request authentication and current actor authority precede private intent/receipt reads, with no existence leakage to unrelated issuers or stores.

## Link-review evidence and separation of people

Provider proof establishes control of an account, not which employee it belongs to. An independent A04.2 owner/admin must review one of these evidence routes, or both, for the exact person, intent and locked provider identity:

| Evidence type | Required facts |
| --- | --- |
| provider_contact_match | The trusted provider has verified the email/phone being compared, and it equals a personal contact recorded for that canonical person by an authorized contact actor before setup issuance. Bind the exact contact record/revision, author/time and reviewed comparison rules captured at issuance; no fuzzy name match, shared-address assumption or browser-supplied verification flag. |
| in_person_attestation | The different-person owner/admin link reviewer personally confirms the actual person and their control of the verified provider account for this intent. Bind the attester person, time, person/intent and locked provider identity. A screenshot, setup code or issuer assurance alone does not attest to account control. |
| both | Record and recheck both routes against the same person/intent/provider binding. |

Audit the evidence type, approved verification outcome, contact reference/revision and recording attribution when used, or attester/time when used, plus the exact intent/provider-binding/proof references and reviewer. Keep raw contact values in their controlled person repository; do not copy credentials, tokens, identity-document images or contact values into public reports, receipts or audit messages. Evidence custody/retention details remain A06/S05 decisions.

A setup issuer has no contact-editing authority merely from that role. If an issuer independently has authority to add/change a contact for this enrollment, its pre-issuance timestamp alone is insufficient: the different-person owner/admin reviewer must independently confirm the person's ownership of that contact or use their own in-person attestation. A later contact change invalidates the captured evidence context and requires fresh review and explicit reissue bound to the new pre-issuance contact revision; never rewrite the old intent/evidence to make an issuer's second account appear to match. Preserve the original provider lock and any unresolved external outcome. Shared/ambiguous contacts cannot be the sole basis for an automatic link. Evidence review never automatically links people by name/email.

The canonical issuer and link approver must differ at final commit. Owning two provider accounts, using two sessions, holding two store memberships or combining owner/admin roles does not create a second person. The approver derives identity from their current verified actor, not a request-selected person ID. If no different eligible approver is available, hold the intent for the separately reviewed A05 bootstrap/recovery procedure; there is no ordinary self-approval fallback. This separation is specific to onboarding link approval; it does not reverse A04.3's allowed reasoned/audited operational self-increases. In-person evidence supplements human-to-account binding and never replaces verified provider authentication or A03's pending-sign-in rule when usable personal email/phone is unavailable.

## First verified provider identity lock

Only a server-verified proof for this exact intent/audience/challenge can initialize the provider identity lock. Coordinate that first binding with the intent revision, and store the configured provider/issuer/tenant namespace plus subject as an immutable tuple for the intent. Unverified claims cannot win the lock. The first binding grants no access and does not itself satisfy the human-link evidence standard.

A fresh valid proof for the same locked tuple may be inspected/retried idempotently under the original context; routine proof refresh does not change the subject. A different subject or provider/issuer/tenant is refused (proposed outcome onboarding_subject_conflict), even before link approval. Two competing first proofs serialize: one tuple can be bound, and the other is refused. Final approval rechecks the binding revision and exact locked tuple, independently of current evidence/authority checks. These are future transaction requirements, not tested concurrency here.

Changing the identity requires explicit authorized reissue into a new intent/approval, with fresh evidence and review; preserve the old tuple, audit and reconciliation references and revoke the superseded local approval. Reissue neither deletes a possibly created provider account nor clears an unknown provider outcome or silently rebinds an existing primary link. Supported provider reconciliation and the separate recovery/rebind workflow still apply.

## Proposed progression and failure contract

1. An authorized issuer requests setup for an already reviewed person within their scope. Refuse an existing active primary provider link in the ordinary first-link flow; route replacement/rebind to separately reviewed recovery. A03 keeps people without usable personal email/phone pending for sign-in while preserving their scheduling/employment/history; do not invent contact information or substitute a shared PIN.
2. In a coordinated app transaction, recheck current issuer authority, person/membership scope, relevant revisions and exact payload, then record the intent/one-use approval and minimal audit/receipt outcome. The setup code approves proceeding with setup; it is not personal authentication and cannot establish the provider subject or create permissions.
3. Before any provider side effect, durably record the specific operation against that intent through the future trusted adapter. Perform provider enrollment/authentication outside SQL locks and retry callbacks. Use only supported provider idempotency/reconciliation guarantees; if those cannot be proven, hold progression rather than create another account after a timeout. Lease/attempt bounds remain A05 selections, not copied constants.
4. Verify provider proof against the exact intent, canonical person proposal, configured provider/issuer/tenant namespace, supported audience/challenge and current validity. The first accepted proof locks that namespace/subject tuple under intent coordination; a different tuple is refused and requires explicit reissue, rather than replacement in the same intent. Browser-selected actor IDs, possession of the setup code, matching email/name or a provider account-creation response alone cannot approve the human binding. A source export never provisions a login or operational authority.
5. An A04.2 owner/admin who is a different canonical person from the setup issuer reviews the person-to-subject match against the defined contact/attestation evidence standard. Under shared coordination, recheck that separation, current reviewer/session, issuer authority/fence, person/link/membership context, approval state, evidence references/revisions, locked provider tuple/binding revision, supported provider proof and exact expected revisions. Enforce one current person per provider identity and at most one active primary link per person, including concurrent intents. Commit link, approval consumption, audit and receipt atomically or none; no operational capability is created. A stale or revoked reviewer/issuer cannot finish from old setup evidence.
6. Reuse the same intent/receipt for retry or uncertain-response inspection after current authorization. Same identity/payload yields the already committed metadata; changed payload conflicts. A committed link is never duplicated by replay. Do not put a raw setup secret in a durable receipt merely to make it replayable. Safe one-time delivery and lost-issuance-response behavior must be selected under A05/A06 before implementation; metadata-only replay with an explicit superseding reissue is a proposal, not an adopted delivery mechanism.
7. Timeout or lost provider response leaves provider=unknown and reconciliation=required. Verify the original operation with the trusted adapter; do not search by name/email to infer a match, create a second human/account, delete a possibly successful account or clear the obligation on expiry. If provider creation succeeded but local authority was lost, preserve the reconciliation record and grant no access. Account cleanup/rebind requires separately authorized identity/recovery review.
8. Expiry/revocation/ordinary reissue invalidates superseded local setup approval under coordination but does not rewrite people, memberships, past actors or an unknown external outcome. Concurrent reissue/redemption must serialize; exactly one valid approval may be consumed for the reviewed link. Exceptions, reauthentication, actual delivery and provider-side cancellation remain explicit A05/A06/provider decisions.

The issuing actor's durable authority/eligibility is revalidated; do not treat routine refresh, last-seen changes or unrelated workspace writes as revocation. Whether expiration of the original issuer browser session affects an already issued approval must be settled in the A05 session contract; no requirement for the issuer to remain continuously logged in is silently introduced. The actor making each current request always needs their own valid verified session. Setup/link access and receipt fences must detect relevant committed revocation before returning private results, using the [separate current final-access check](SOURCE_NORMALIZATION_TARGET_READ_DESIGN.md).

Do not attach custom lifecycle triggers to provider-managed auth.sessions. No SQL, lock, retry, provider call or concurrency test is implemented by this design.

Audit the canonical actor/person, exact scope/action, reason, expected/current revisions, proposal/intent/receipt/operation references, evidence type and referenced contact/attestation/provider binding, and approved before/after link or grant metadata. Include authority basis, self-grant increment, admin-enablement epoch, suspension and explicit owner reconfirmation provenance where relevant. Current display names come from the shared employee projection; a historical name is labelled name at time of entry. Setup issuers receive only their authorized setup status, not other-store rosters, contacts, unrelated owner records or provider credentials. Operator diagnostics and real intake reports stay outside Git/PR artifacts; use only fictional public fixtures and aggregate evidence. Transactional notice intent does not prove delivery.
