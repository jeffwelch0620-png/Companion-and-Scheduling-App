## Separate the five decisions

| Decision | Required authority/evidence | What it does not grant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person identity and provider link | Separate reviewed identity workflow with a verified provider subject and approved person match | Employment, source-review designation, operational permissions or ownership |
| Store membership and jobs | Explicit reviewed employment/store association and configured jobs | Sign-in, account linking, capabilities or training clearance merely from a job title |
| Setup approval | Narrow authorized issuer and one-use approval for an already approved person/onboarding intent; exact issuer policy A04 pending | Provider identity by itself, account rebind, owner/admin status or permission grants |
| Ordinary operational grants | A01 owner/enabled-admin authority; exact capability/store/department scope, revisions, reason and current identity | Owner seat, admin enablement, recovery powers or source certification merely from a role template |
| Protected owner/admin changes | Owner-only admin switch; A02 different-owner approval for owner-set changes; separate reviewed bootstrap/recovery | Infrastructure/operator authority, provider dashboard or repository access |

A verified provider login does not automatically activate a person, approve membership or assign permissions. A Toast identifier, source email, name, setup code or imported manager/title never substitutes for that approval. An owner or separately designated non-employed reviewer needs a person/account actor, not fabricated employment.

## Proposed onboarding and link lifecycle

These are logical contracts and future acceptance requirements, not installed tables, command names or implemented provider APIs.

1. Create an onboarding intent for an explicitly reviewed person and intended scope. Record issuer/reviewer attribution, relevant person/member/input revisions, reason and receipt identity. If an existing verified link is present, ordinary setup reissue cannot replace it; route to the separate recovery/rebind review.
2. Issue a bounded, one-use setup approval through an authorized issuer under A04. Store only an appropriate secret hash, validity/attempt state and necessary audit metadata; do not log tokens, passwords or PINs. Short validity and bounded attempts are proposed safeguards whose exact settings remain A05 decisions.
3. Obtain provider enrollment/authentication proof through a supported, separately verified integration. A setup approval is a linking/onboarding approval, not a replacement personal authentication system. Matching email/name or possessing a code alone does not establish the canonical human/provider-subject link.
4. Propose and explicitly review the provider subject-to-person binding in the identity workflow. Use the verified server subject, not a request-selected actor. The exact identity-review/delegation predicate is still subject to A04/I07; do not silently assign that power to every manager or source reviewer.
5. Commit the approved link through coordinated shared writes with exact expected revisions and unique subject/person bindings. One verified provider identity (configured provider/issuer namespace plus subject, including tenant where required) maps to one canonical person in the current binding; each person has at most one active primary provider link. A subject cannot concurrently bind to two people, and a person cannot acquire a second active primary link through setup reissue. Account merges and rebinds require the separately reviewed exception workflow, coordinated uniqueness checks, session invalidation and retained historical attribution; historical bindings are not active sign-in links. Linking does not copy a source capability list. Operational access remains held until independent membership/grant reviews are satisfied; independent means a separate authorization decision, not an unselected two-person requirement for every ordinary onboarding.
6. Maintain an explicit intent state such as approved, provider-pending, provider-confirmed, link-review-pending, linked, refused or reconciliation-required. These are proposed state labels. An unknown provider outcome must be reconciled through the original intent, not retried by creating another person or account.

Provider creation/recovery side effects occur outside the SQL transaction retry callback. Application intent transitions and receipts make local progress reviewable; a provider timeout does not prove success or failure. Revalidate current issuer/reviewer authority and exact person/link revisions before any final commit. If access is revoked after a provider account was created, preserve the reconciliation obligation and grant no operational access. Provider cleanup is a separately authorized, identity-verified operation; never delete an account on an ambiguous timeout.

A03 does not archive the employee, set scheduleOnly automatically, remove jobs/leave/history or fabricate an email/phone. Existing valid records remain available under authorized manager/scheduling reads. No shared store PIN, manager impersonation or employee-name selector is substituted for personal sign-in. If a supported no-contact flow is designed later, link through the same person/alias and audit contracts without rewriting historical actors.

## Ordinary access administration and administrator switch

A01 is enforced in the shared policy, not separately in each interface. Both trusted backends derive the actor from verified current person/session/link state. A role/title/imported capability array is never grant evidence. Every proposed grant records exact registered capability and organization/store/optional department scope, current resource/target revisions, actor, reason and effective state. Unknown scopes/capabilities fail closed.

Operational grants do not modify owner seats, administrator enablement or source-review designation levels. Those retain their protected workflows. Role templates may prepare proposals; they do not provision capabilities by default. schedule.correct remains separate and initially granted to nobody; owner/admin eligibility is not a default correction grant or an override of person-level independence requirements.

Disabling the administrator switch removes its organization-policy path immediately while preserving independent ordinary grants/designations according to their own state. Only an owner controls that switch; an admin cannot enable themselves or change owners. Ending one store membership removes that store's employment-dependent operational eligibility while preserving another approved membership and independently designated non-employed reviewer access. Preserve PR63's last-membership advisory-only rule and person/account eligibility lapse handling.

Every store employment-dependent grant captures the current membership epoch for that person/store. Creation requires current approved, non-schedule-only employment eligibility under the same coordination as membership changes; no pre-staged grant may become effective merely because a pending/ended member is later approved. Rehire needs an explicit fresh grant review. Approved scheduling participation and schedule-only state remain separate: operational employment eligibility is approved && !scheduleOnly. Moving an eligible member to schedule-only loses that eligibility and advances the epoch even if scheduling participation stays approved. Returning from schedule-only retains the advanced epoch and requires a fresh grant; it never revives old grants. Ending or otherwise losing approved employment eligibility advances this monotonic epoch in the same coordinated transaction and records the actor, reason and before/after epoch in the audit. The epoch is not reset or reused when the same durable membership UUID is re-approved. Old grants remain inactive even after re-approval; a current A01-authorized actor must explicitly review and re-grant against the new epoch. No-op writes, routine profile/job edits that preserve employment eligibility and token refresh do not advance this epoch. Current authorization requires both approved employment and the grant's captured epoch matching the membership's current epoch. Coordinate grant creation, membership ending and re-approval to prevent a concurrent grant retaining old authority. This store-employment rule does not revoke another store's grants, organization owner/admin policy paths or independent source-review designations; their own lifecycle rules still apply.

No new self-elevation policy is inferred from A01. A04's proposed no-self-increase rule must be explicitly settled before implementing grant commands that could increase the actor's own independent permissions. No restriction here changes an owner's already selected organization-policy authority. Emergency/owner recovery, access delegation beyond A01 and infrastructure credentials remain separate decisions.

## Protected owner changes and recovery

Under A02, a verified current owner proposes an exact owner-set change; a different currently eligible existing owner approves that exact proposal. Compare canonical person UUIDs, not memberships, browser sessions or provider accounts. Bind the target, change, reason, current owner-set revision, proposal creation/expiry times and approval context. Approval applies the change atomically in the same coordinated transaction after acquiring the relevant locks and rechecking both actors' current eligibility/ownership, unchanged proposal/owner-set revision and remaining eligible owners. Persist approval, owner-set mutation, audit and receipt together, or none of them; there is no durable approved-but-unapplied state or later applier. Check expiry against current server time after locks, not the request's arrival time. A stale-by-time proposal, revoked approver or changed target/context is refused and requires a new proposal/review. The bounded proposal validity duration must be explicitly selected under A05 before implementation; no expiry value is implied by this design.

The approval requirement does not silently require a third owner: proposer and approver must differ from each other. For a removal proposed by another owner, the removal target may supply the second-owner approval only through their explicit, audited consent to that exact proposal while still an eligible existing owner. For voluntary self-removal, the departing owner may propose their own removal but another eligible existing owner must approve; there is no unilateral self-removal exception. A proposed new owner is not yet an existing owner and cannot supply the second-owner approval. Preserve at least one eligible owner after any ordinary change; no ordinary command may remove the last owner or treat an inactive historical seat as sufficient recovery coverage.

With only one eligible owner, even adding a second owner cannot satisfy ordinary A02 approval. Single-owner addition and contested removal are explicitly routed to A05, alongside initial owner roster/bootstrap and loss-of-last-owner recovery.

Contested means an explicit, recorded objection by the removal target to that exact proposal. Absence of consent, including silence, is not an objection and is not consent. The target objection hold applies in organizations with three or more owners: even if two other eligible owners propose and approve, a recorded objection prevents ordinary removal and routes it to A05. In a two-owner organization, a removal without the target's consent also cannot meet ordinary A02; do not infer permission for a sole-owner override or coercive removal. A05 must select the exceptional procedure, approvers and evidence before any such action. No first-login ownership, shared recovery PIN or repository-key shortcut is permitted.

Recheck recorded objections under the same coordination as atomic approval/application, so an objection committed before the final check cannot be bypassed by a concurrent approval. In a three-owner organization, A may propose removing C and B may approve while C is silent: A and B can satisfy the distinct-existing-owner rule without C's consent, provided no recorded objection exists and all other checks pass. Target notification and any response-window requirements must be explicitly settled under A05 before implementing removal; silence does not select a zero-length window or permission to skip notification. A later objection does not retroactively undo a committed removal and requires the separately reviewed A05 process. This draft does not authorize operator intervention or create recovery identities.

Bootstrap must use verified real provider/person IDs and an explicitly approved initial owner roster after the tested Inventory/catalog handoff. If a second owner is intended, that reviewed initial bootstrap roster can include both owners, rather than relying on an ordinary single-owner addition that cannot meet A02. Candidate fictional subjects and rehearsal booleans are not bootstrap evidence. Recovery/rebind must independently verify the human, review current bindings, invalidate superseded sessions/eligibility as appropriate and retain person/history IDs. The exact evidence, operator/owner approvals and recovery-notice recipients remain A05/A06 decisions, not a workflow silently selected by merging this document.

## Sessions, eligibility and logout

The future adapter must validate current provider proof and application session/person/link state on every relevant request, including receipt reads/replays. Specify per-application audience/session behavior and cross-interface logout guarantees before exposing routes; do not share raw credentials or assume copying a browser cookie integrates the interfaces.

Apply the accepted monotonic person/account-link/organization/store eligibility counters and explicit lifecycle evidence from PR62/PR63. Provider-side lapse/re-enable requires trusted lifecycle reconciliation; currently valid credentials alone do not prove continuity. A designee's captured/current eligibility vectors must agree before source reads/certification. These rules do not turn ordinary token refresh into a lapse: session.revision advances for semantic access/session-invalidation changes, not refresh, expiry renewal or last-seen updates.

Ordinary logout/expiry ends that session without revoking a durable source designation. A fresh verified session may reuse an otherwise eligible designation. Person/link/organization/store eligibility lapse instead invalidates relevant captured vectors; re-enable does not silently restore them. Own-designation self-revocation remains available with valid personal identity/session and personal lifecycle evidence even for an inactive organization/store; immutable scope and record ownership still match.

Read target data through a consistent authorized snapshot and a separate current final-access/session check. A revocation committed before that final check discards the assembled private result. This cannot retract already delivered data or cover a commit after the final check; every subsequent request rechecks. Routine global workspace activity does not become a session/access revision.

## Coordination, audit and privacy

Future grant/link/owner/admin/session-lifecycle writes use the single shared ledger's reviewed lock order and explicit definitions. Coordinate affected organization/store/person scopes before mutation, revalidate scope sets after locks, and retain whole-transaction SQLSTATE 40001 retries with the same receipt/payload. No custom triggers on provider-managed auth.sessions and no provider side effect inside the retry callback.

Authentication/current authority precedes private receipt retrieval; then check a matching receipt before mutable business validation for a new command. Same receipt/payload replays one committed outcome, changed payload conflicts, and a revoked actor cannot retrieve a private historical result just by knowing its ID. Semantic no-ops preserve relevant revisions; already-revoked relinquishment may return noop:true. No new command implementation is supplied here.

Audit person actors, optional acting membership, exact scope/action, before/after revisions/state, reason, relevant proposal/approval/receipt IDs and times. Display current names from the controlled person projection; entry-name snapshots are labelled name at time of entry. Preserve durable person/history when login/employment ends. Never turn a shared legacy PIN/name attribution into a guessed verified actor.

Contact/setup/recovery metadata and operator reports are sensitive. Exclude them from Git/PR artifacts, logs and unrestricted browser storage. Raw login/setup secrets, passwords, refresh tokens and credential snapshots are never archived as source evidence. Review audit/retention/deletion rules under A06/I08/S05; do not infer a 30-day cleanup rule or copy the current D1 session lifetime. Transactional notice intents are not notification delivery.

| Three owners: A proposes removing C, B approves, and C is silent | Silence is neither consent nor objection; A and B satisfy the two-owner approval rule if no objection is recorded and all current authority, revision, expiry, remaining-owner and A05-selected notification/response checks pass. No implicit waiting-period value is selected. |
| Target objection commits while another owner is waiting to approve removal | Coordinated final check detects the recorded objection and refuses ordinary removal; no partial owner mutation/approval. An objection after committed removal does not retroactively undo it. |
| Non-last owner voluntarily proposes their own removal | Another currently eligible existing owner must approve; no unilateral removal, and at least one eligible owner remains. |
| Proposal passes its A05-selected expiry before approval or while waiting for coordination | Current server-time check refuses the stale proposal, with no approval/owner mutation committed; require a fresh proposal/review. |
| Approved initial bootstrap intends two owners | Both verified people may be included in the reviewed bootstrap roster under the selected A05 procedure; neither is auto-created from a first login. |
| Proposal changes, approver loses ownership, or last eligible owner would be removed | Refuse; no stale approval or last-owner removal. |
| Only one eligible owner remains, or initial bootstrap is needed | Ordinary owner-set change remains held; separately reviewed A05 procedure required, without automatic bypass. |
| Membership ends then is re-approved with the same UUID | Ending atomically advances the store employment epoch and audits it; old grants stay inactive after re-approval until explicitly reviewed/re-granted at the new epoch. Other stores and independent reviewer authority retain their own rules. |
| Grant is proposed for a pending, ended or schedule-only membership | Refuse pre-staging at creation/commit. On rehire or return to operational eligibility, require a new explicit grant review at the retained current epoch. |
| Approved operational member becomes schedule-only, then returns | Advance epoch when operational eligibility is lost; no grant is effective while schedule-only, and old grants remain inactive on return until explicitly re-granted. |
