## Owner-proposal timing invariant

For an owner removal, define P as proposal validity from creation, D as the maximum delivery-confirmation window from creation and R as the minimum response interval after the final required recipient's confirmed delivery. All are positive bounded durations in the same unit, calculated using trusted server time. Require **P > D + R**, with safe arithmetic and explicit configuration validation. Equality, negative/zero values, overflow and missing bounds are refused; no proposal may be created under invalid configuration.

The proposed values satisfy the rule: 7 days > 24 hours + 48 hours. If delivery is not confirmed by D, the proposal stays unable to apply and expires; use a fresh reviewed proposal rather than shortening R or silently extending P. A substantive payload/recipient change invalidates the old review/notice context. At application recheck both absolute proposal expiry and the completed response interval; a valid configuration alone does not prove a particular proposal is still valid. This is a future validation requirement; no configuration code is added by this docs-only PR.

## Emergency access suspension — SEC10 proposal

Suspension is a separate, reversible containment state for a canonical person in the affected organization. The initiating actor must be a different current eligible owner, or a governance approver whose suspension authority has been explicitly selected; ordinary enabled admin, the removal proposer title or infrastructure-key possession alone supplies no such authority. Require fresh verified actor proof, a mandatory case, concrete compromise reason/evidence references, expected revisions, exact scope, review deadline and audit. An already suspended/ineligible actor cannot initiate a reciprocal suspension through that stale authority.

Atomically establish an application-side suspension fence, invalidate affected application sessions and block new sessions, protected actions and private receipt/read results for that scope. Every server path and any direct database access policy must observe the fence, including the final current-access check. Do not expose the feature until all supported paths enforce it. Provider sign-out/revocation is additional reconciliation outside SQL retry callbacks; it is not sufficient for immediate containment because an issued JWT can remain valid until expiry, as documented by [Supabase signOut](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/auth-signout). Keep provider-wide side effects explicit if they would affect unrelated organizations.

Suspension grants no access, transfers no ownership, changes no owner roster and does not clear an objection, grant, captured epoch or admin self-grant obligation. A suspended owner's recorded objection continues to hold ordinary removal. Preserve an independently verified out-of-band route to submit evidence/objections and request review; suspended app sessions cannot be used to perform privileged actions through an appeal screen.

Notify the target and other current owners promptly with minimal case/status details, and escalate delivery failure through governance. Notification failure must not prevent immediate containment, unlike the ordinary removal waiting window. Keep case evidence private. The initiator cannot independently lift or extend their own suspension decision, erase its audit, change ownership or reuse repeated case IDs to reset review deadlines. An independent eligible owner or selected governance approver must review evidence, actor conflicts, necessity and scope, and record a reasoned lift/continuation with its own bounded deadline. If no independent eligible owner is available, use the selected governance path; invent no ordinary admin fallback.

Recommended review: within 4 hours, with explicit independent continuation/lift at least every 24 hours. Missed review triggers a visible escalated case and governance intervention, not automatic recovery of suspect credentials or silent rolling renewal by the initiator. Case ownership, escalation recipient and final maximum/escalation disposition remain owner selections; until selected and tested, emergency suspension cannot be rolled out. A lift requires current independent proof and a reviewed safe recovery state, advances semantic suspension/session revisions and permits only fresh eligible sessions. It does not resurrect revoked tokens or stale grants/designations; any lifecycle lapse still obeys the accepted epoch rules.

## Infrastructure break-glass — SEC11 proposal

Before production, record the named DB/provider credential custodians, vault/backup custody and the recovery trustee's verified identity/contact in a controlled private register, never Git or this public PR. Keep human operator accounts separate from application/service identities and app owners/admins. Protect operator accounts with supported MFA, least privilege and audited access. Service credentials remain in approved secret storage; they are not shared employee credentials, recovery PINs or a way to make an operator an app owner.

An optional sealed runbook must be separately approved, versioned and tested. Define its incident prerequisites, named authorizer/executor, two-person access/approval where feasible, independent review, allowed actions, expiry, evidence and credential rotation/resealing after use. If only one person can operate, the exceptional authorization and independent post-review must be explicitly selected; infrastructure availability does not silently authorize bypassing the two-person design. Do not place secrets or seal-opening instructions in public artifacts.

**Direct writes outside the reviewed path are an incident, not a recovery method.** Even a reviewed emergency intervention must record every direct write with incident ID, canonical human operator, time, reason, approved script/version, affected scope, before/after evidence, approvers and result in an access-controlled audit location outside the database being repaired. Never log credential values. Capture evidence and contain an unauthorized write rather than disguising it as normal recovery or claiming its current counters prove continuity.

Before reopening access after intervention, reconcile the actual owner roster, objections and suspension cases; provider subjects/account links; person/account-link/organization/store eligibility counters; membership/grant epochs; admin enablement epochs and person-scoped self-grant obligations; sessions and stale provider tokens; source-review/designation fences; and receipts/outbox/reconciliation obligations. Use trusted pre-incident evidence and monotonic floors: never restore old counters or claim a guessed increment repairs an unknown history. If continuity cannot be established, keep affected access held and require fresh reviewed grants/designations through the selected incident path. Preserve historical person/record identities.

Invalidate/reconcile affected sessions through supported provider and application paths, rotate exposed credentials, rerun integrity/access checks and obtain independent case closure. Restoration or a provider-console edit does not itself prove recovery. All of this remains a proposed runbook and future acceptance work; no credential is accessed, direct write performed or operator appointed by this document.

## Proposed sensitive-action proof contract

This section depends on SEC01/SEC02; it defines no installed endpoint or token format. A future trusted adapter must verify the provider, audience and subject, resolve the current canonical person/session and capture supported fresh-factor evidence. A browser-supplied AAL, boolean, timestamp, factor screenshot or second account is insufficient. Bind the proof to canonical actor, organization, action, exact payload/proposal digest, relevant semantic revisions and a selected expiry. A changed action/target/payload needs fresh review. Do not claim every provider JWT's issue time proves a fresh factor challenge.

At application, coordinate the shared scopes, check current authority, evidence/freshness, target eligibility and exact revisions, then consume any one-use application proof and commit business change/audit/receipt atomically. Provider side effects occur outside SQL locks and retry callbacks. A committed receipt can replay its authorized metadata without rerunning the provider operation; changed payload conflicts, and a revoked actor cannot inspect it. A stale or expired proposal is refused rather than silently extended. Selection of bounded timings does not certify provider idempotency or solve distributed atomicity.

Revocation, personal logout and self-relinquishment require explicit SEC01 treatment so a lost factor cannot force continued access. Any limited safety path must be separately specified by exact subject/scope, cannot grant or broaden access, must retain reason/audit and must not become a replacement account-recovery path. Until selected, this draft creates no exemption to existing checks.

## Proposed exceptional case sequence

1. Record a minimal private case for one organization/person/action. Capture the accepted source revisions and evidence references, without credentials or an invented employment membership. A case ID confers no access.
2. Identify the independently verified people proposed as governance approvers and technical executor under the separately selected SEC05/SEC06 rules. Bind their current authority and conflicts of interest. Infrastructure access alone cannot count as app-owner consent. If the selected approvers/evidence are unavailable, remain held; do not invent a fallback.
3. Preserve the ordinary A02 route wherever it can succeed. For an explicit objection or missing distinct eligible owner, ordinary application remains refused. Document the exceptional scope and governance disposition separately; a support ticket is not consent and an objection is not withdrawn by timeout.
4. Review exact proposed owner/person/link changes, target notification and response evidence, current roster and source keys. Reverify immediately before application under the shared lock order. A material change stales the case; no SQL script with a guessed person ID is an approved bootstrap roster.
5. Use an explicitly reviewed application path in Inventory's single ledger after the tested catalog handoff. Apply only the approved scope, with current-state checks, audit and receipt; preserve the original person/history. An executor gains no durable ownership or ordinary grant from executing the case.
6. Independently verify the resulting access/link/session state, lifecycle invalidation and minimal owner notice intents. Provider reconciliation and actual notice delivery remain separate obligations. Refusal or ambiguity grants nothing and keeps the case evidence/holds. No such application path is implemented or authorized by this draft.
